Meticulously Crafted

Designed for how you actually work.

FoldyDock combines the familiarity of iOS folder organization with the raw power and fluidity of modern macOS.

📂

iOS-Style App Folders with Live Miniatures

Tired of a cluttered 50-app dock? Drag applications together to create clean folders. The miniature grid automatically recalculates (2x2, 3x3, 4x4) with sub-pixel alignment. Click to reveal an elegant popover with drag-and-drop reordering.

🪟

Real-time Frosted Glass

Native `NSVisualEffectView` provides instant hardware-accelerated background blur. Works seamlessly with Dynamic Wallpapers, dark mode, and transparent windows.

✨
HUD Vibrancy & Soft Drop Shadows
🌐

Present on All Spaces

FoldyDock uses `.canJoinAllSpaces` window collection behavior. Whether you have 2 or 10 virtual desktops or full-screen apps, your dock is always right where you need it.

⚡

Window Count Activity Dots

Know at a glance how many windows are open: 1 dot, 2 dots, 3 dots, or 2 dots with a mini `+` indicator for 4 or more instances.

🖱️

1-Click App Termination

Middle-click any running app (or sub-app inside a closed folder!) to quit it instantly. No more digging through Activity Monitor or right-click menus.

⚙️

Dedicated Settings Window & Instant Customization

Press ⌘ , or right-click the dock to tailor icon sizes (32 to 96 pt), vertical & horizontal paddings, autohide delays, hidden app opacity, and launch at login.

Fluid Controls

Speed at your fingertips.

Designed from the ground up to respect Apple's human interface guidelines with snappy physical feedback.

Left Click
Launch & Focus
Launches app or brings it to the front with an authentic spring bounce animation.
Middle Click
Instant Quit
Terminates the clicked application instantly with a quick fade-and-shrink dismissal.
Drag & Drop
Group into Folder
Drop an application icon onto another to instantly group them into a dynamic folder.
⌘ , Shortcut
Open Preferences
Access the native settings panel to configure margins, sizes, and startup behavior.
